Application information

QRoll – Smart Attendance Management System

Academic attendance management concept inspired by the supplied QRoll – Attendance Register reference, associated with Dr. Vikas Panthi, School of Computing Science and Engineering (SCOPE), VIT Bhopal University.

This is an independent browser prototype, not the original source application and not an official institutional deployment.

Reference features observed

The uploaded source shows an attendance register with date-wise P/A/blank marking, saved search/filter/sort views, subject sheets, date-column actions, local browser persistence, undoable edits, XLSX/CSV export, dashboard and cohort analytics, live rotating QR presentation, projection mode, camera/photo scanning, check-in feedback, and activity/attempt logging. Its QR signature and storage are implemented client-side; this prototype does not treat them as production security.

Deployment architecture recommendation

Production: React + TypeScript client; FastAPI or NestJS REST service; PostgreSQL with migrations; Argon2id password hashes; role-based authorization; server-signed short-lived QR tokens, one-time replay protection, rate limits, immutable audit events, validated XLSX imports, scheduled encrypted backups, and HTTPS deployment. Tables: users, roles, students, programmes, subjects, terms, sections, enrolments, attendance_sessions, attendance_records, qr_tokens, scan_attempts, audit_logs, import_jobs, settings.

QRoll prototype · Browser data is stored locally on this device. Do not enter sensitive real student data.
